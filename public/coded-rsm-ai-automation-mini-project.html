Your scenarioSenior associate, Audit and AssuranceTask 2.6 · 85 min

Audit and Assurance

Mirqab's audit team keeps one findings log for 12 clients: 30 findings so far. Every Sunday the partner asks three questions: what is still open, what is overdue, and which high-risk findings need her. Today a supervisor spends about 40 minutes filtering the sheet and writing the email.

Your job today: build an agent that does this work for the team, shows the result as an artifact, and runs on a schedule. A person still reviews every run. Everything you need is on this page, in five steps.

Heads up: this file hides two problems. A good agent finds and flags them. It never fixes them.

Your file
mirqab-audit-findings-log-2026.xlsx

30 rows · Finding, Client, Area, Risk, Owner, Raised, Due, Status

The artifactA findings dashboard: tiles for open, overdue and high-risk open; a chart by area; a table by owner; a filter by client.
When and whoEvery Sunday at 08:00. Reviewed by the engagement supervisor.
What the agent must do
  • List every finding that is not Closed, grouped by client.
  • List the overdue ones: due before today and not Closed.
  • List the high-risk findings that are not Closed, first.
  • Write one follow-up line for each owner.
  • Flag any data problem. Never fix it.

Your scenarioAnalyst, Corporate FinanceTask 2.6 · 85 min

Corporate Finance

The Corporate Finance partners review the deal pipeline every Monday: 22 deals, from leads to final reports. The sheet has no totals, no weighted value and no view by stage, so an analyst builds it again every week.

Your job today: build an agent that does this work for the team, shows the result as an artifact, and runs on a schedule. A person still reviews every run. Everything you need is on this page, in five steps.

Heads up: this file hides two problems. A good agent finds and flags them. It never fixes them.

Your file
mirqab-cf-deal-pipeline-2026.xlsx

22 rows · Deal, Client, Service, Sector, Stage, Fee estimate (KWD), Probability (%), Expected close, Lead partner

The artifactA pipeline dashboard, then a 5-slide update for the partners' meeting.
When and whoEvery Monday at 07:30. Reviewed by the lead partner.
What the agent must do
  • Total the fee estimates, and the weighted value (fee × probability) by stage.
  • List the deals expected to close in the next 30 days.
  • List stale deals: the close date has passed and the stage is still Proposal.
  • Leave a deal with a blank probability out of the weighted value, and flag it.
  • Never guess a probability.
