AI Innovation and Automation · CODED × RSM · Sunday 15 and Monday 16 November 2026 · 08:30-16:00 · CODED, Alsharekh classroom (Frontend Campus). CODED × RSM Albazie client program. 18 participants: Assurance 12, Corporate Finance 2, Support 3, Analytics Advisory 1. They use AI already; Claude may be new. Story: Mirqab & Co. and Abdali Foods (fictional). Tools: Claude: chat, Projects, the Google Drive connector, artifacts, skills and scheduled tasks. n8n as a trainer demo. Habit: Claude does the first pass. You review and sign off.
Outcomes
  1. I can explain what generative AI does well and where it can be wrong, and check its output against the source.
  2. I can find the tasks in my work that suit an agent, and describe one as an Agent Card: input, instruction, output and when.
  3. I can build a Claude Project with instructions and knowledge that gives the same, checkable result on every run.
  4. I can connect an agent to a Drive folder and have it return its output in a fixed format.
  5. I can package my method as a skill and run it on a schedule, with a human review step.
  6. I can match each Claude feature to its equivalent in ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Gemini.
