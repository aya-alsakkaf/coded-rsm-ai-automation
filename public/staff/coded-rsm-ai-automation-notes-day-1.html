09What it really does.Lesson 1 · AI foundations · term

24Five parts of a good prompt.Lesson 2 · Why chat isn't enough · list
  • SaySo here is the recipe, in five parts. Role: tell Claude who it is and what the job is. Steps: the order of the work. Rules: the source to use, the units and the dates. 29From a chat to an agent.Lesson 2 · Why chat isn't enough · term
  • SaySo what is an agent? It is AI that you prepare for one job, and it does that job identically each time. Its instructions are saved, so you never type them again. It can reach the files it needs. It always gives back the same shape. And from tomorrow, it can run on a schedule, without you. Think of a trained associate with a written procedure and access to the client file. A chat answers a question. An agent does a job. Today you build your first one, piece by piece.
30Which tasks suit an agent?Lesson 2 · Why chat isn't enough · term
  • SayNot every task should become an agent. Ask three questions about a task. Does it come back every week? Are the rules clear enough to write down? Does a document go in, and a document come out? Three yes answers, and you have a candidate. Anything that needs judgment, or a meeting with a client, stays with you. Pick the task first, and the tool second.
