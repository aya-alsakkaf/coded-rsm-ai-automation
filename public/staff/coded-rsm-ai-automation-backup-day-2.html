Lesson 1 · Skills · Task 2.1

A skill for your own task

Claude · skills

A good skill has

  • A short name in lowercase with hyphens, for example lease-key-terms.
  • One line on when to use it: the task and the file type.
  • Steps, rules, the output columns in order, and When unsure.
  • No client or firm names, and no real figures.

Lesson 5 · Mini project · Task 2.6

Mini project: your department's agent

Claude · everything

The department packs

Audit and Assurance · mirqab-audit-findings-log-2026.xlsx

  • Partners ask every week which findings are still open, which are overdue and which high-risk ones need them.
  • Agent: Reads the findings log and lists open, overdue and high-risk findings, with a follow-up line for each owner.
  • Artifact: A findings dashboard: open by risk, overdue count, by area and by owner.
  • When: Every Sunday at 08:00, from the log in the team's Inbox.

Tax · mirqab-tax-filing-calendar-2026.xlsx

  • The tax team keeps one calendar for every client filing. A missed date means a penalty for the client.
  • Agent: Reads the calendar and lists filings due in the next 14 days, filings past due, and filings with no reviewer.
  • Artifact: A deadline dashboard: due this week, next 30 days, past due, and status by type.
  • When: Every weekday at 08:00.

Corporate Finance · mirqab-cf-deal-pipeline-2026.xlsx

  • Partners review the pipeline every Monday, but the sheet has no totals, no weighted value and no view by stage.
  • Agent: Reads the pipeline and returns the weighted value by stage, deals closing this month, and stale deals.
  • Artifact: A pipeline dashboard and a 5-slide partner update.
  • When: Every Monday at 07:30.

Risk Consulting · mirqab-risk-register-2026.xlsx

  • A client's board wants the top risks on one page, with the risks nobody reviewed this year.
  • Agent: Reads the register, recalculates every score, and lists the top risks and the risks not reviewed in 12 months.
  • Artifact: A heat map (likelihood by impact) and a one-page board summary.
  • When: On the first Sunday of each month.

Cybersecurity · mirqab-cyber-vulnerability-scan-nov.xlsx

  • The scan finds the same issues every week. Management wants to know what is critical, what is old and who owns it.
  • Agent: Reads the scan and lists open Critical and High findings, findings open over 30 days, and the count by owner.
  • Artifact: A risk dashboard: open by severity, age buckets, and the top 5 to fix this week.
  • When: Every Thursday at 07:00, after the weekly scan.

IT and the Technology Hub · mirqab-it-helpdesk-tickets-oct.xlsx

  • The IT manager writes a monthly service report by hand from the ticket export.
  • Agent: Reads the ticket export and returns SLA met by priority, tickets by category, and every breach with its hours.
  • Artifact: A service dashboard and a one-page monthly report.
  • When: On the 1st of each month at 08:00.

ESG and Sustainability · mirqab-esg-energy-emissions-2025.xlsx

  • A client needs its first sustainability report. The data comes from three sites, by month, and nobody has checked it.
  • Agent: Reads the monthly data, calculates emissions with the factors in the file (electricity 0.6 kg CO2e per kWh, diesel 2.68 kg CO2e per litre), and flags gaps and outliers.
  • Artifact: An emissions dashboard by site and month, and a 5-slide summary for the client's board.
  • When: On the 5th of each month, when the site data arrives.

AI and Data Analytics · mirqab-data-sales-transactions-oct.xlsx

  • A retail client wants to know what sells, where and when, and whether its sales data can be trusted.
  • Agent: Checks the data first (duplicates, amount equals quantity times price), then reports sales by branch, category and week.
  • Artifact: A sales dashboard with filters, and a data-quality note on page one.
  • When: Every Sunday at 09:00, on last week's export.

Finance · mirqab-finance-budget-vs-actual-2026.xlsx

  • Every month the finance team explains the variances to the partners, line by line, in a long email.
  • Agent: Calculates the variance in KWD and percent for every line, flags lines over 10% and over KWD 1,000, and drafts one question per flag.
  • Artifact: A variance dashboard by cost center and a one-page memo to the managing partner.
  • When: On the 3rd working day of each month.

Human Resources · mirqab-hr-people-data-2026.xlsx

  • HR answers the same questions every month: headcount, training hours, leave balances and who is on notice.
  • Agent: Reads the people file and returns headcount by department and grade, training hours under the 20-hour target, and leave balances to check.
  • Artifact: An HR dashboard and a one-page monthly note for the partners. Employee IDs only, never names.
  • When: On the last working day of each month.

Customer Experience · mirqab-cx-client-survey-2026.xlsx

  • Client feedback arrives every week, but nobody turns it into a score or a list of what to fix.
  • Agent: Calculates the NPS by service line, groups the comments into themes, and lists the three things to fix first.
  • Artifact: An NPS dashboard and a 5-slide quarterly client-feedback deck.
  • When: Every Sunday at 08:00.
