Step 9

The messy sample and the clock (Task 1.10)

Minutes by hand, minutes with the agent
Answer
  • A supplier invoice in USD with no invoice number: Flag: no invoice number, and the amount is in USD. Do not convert it.
  • A lease with no end date: Flag: no end date. Ask for the term before you schedule it.
  • A trial balance with one prior-year figure missing: Flag: the movement cannot be calculated. Do not treat the blank as zero.
  • An email that answers two requests in one line: Mark both items Outstanding with the client's promised date, and add one follow-up for each.
  • A status sheet with a blank budget: Flag: the budget is blank, so over or under budget cannot be calculated.
