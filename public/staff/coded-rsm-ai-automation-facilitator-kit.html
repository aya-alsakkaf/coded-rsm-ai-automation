Day 1 · Sun 15 Nov 2026 · 08:30-16:30

Designing AI Agents for Real Work

33 slides · about 372 minutes of slide time · breaks and lunch as in the run of show.

01Opening · Title · 1 min

AI Innovation and Automation Day 1 · Designing AI Agents for Real Work

On screen as people arrive. Check that every laptop is signed in to Claude and that the practice files open. Start on time: the opener needs no setup from the room.

02Opening · Opener · two instructions · 10 min

Same tool. Same file. Two instructions.

Press → to send the weak instruction, then → again for the designed one. Ask the room: which reply can you act on? Which one checked the license date? Do not explain the five parts yet; that is block 2. Fallback: prompts 1.1 and 1.2 run live in Claude with the same file.

03Opening · Outcomes · 2 min

By the end of Day 1, you will be able to…

Read the five lines fast. Point at the last one: we test before we trust.

04Opening · Roadmap · 3 min

One firm. Two clients. One agent each.

Make clear that the firm and both clients are fictional, and that no real client data enters any tool today. Show where the files are: the program site, Sample files.

05Opening · MAP test · before · 10 min

Scan the QR code. 10 minutes.

Start the timer. Walk the room and help with the QR code. Say again: no AI, no search, a guess is fine. Fallback: the short link /pre on the program site.

06Reframe · Block 1 · Reframing work · 2 min

Most of the work is not the judgment. It is the moving.

Ask: how much of your last week was judgment, and how much was moving information from one place to another? Take two answers.

07Reframe · How an LLM writes · 12 min

It writes by guessing the next word.

Press → three times: the guesses, then the file, then the rule. A large language model (LLM) predicts the next likely words. Every guess sounds sure. This is why we always give the file and a rule for what to do when the answer is not there.

08Reframe · Chat, agent, workflow · 12 min

Chat, agent, workflow: three steps up.

Most people use step 1 only. Ask who has saved a prompt they reuse: that is the start of step 2.

09Reframe · Where the hours go · 13 min

Where the hours go in audit and onboarding.

Ask each table to add one task that is not on the slide. Write two of them on the board for the sort that follows.

10Reframe · Four questions · 10 min

Four questions find a good candidate.

Run one task from the board through the four questions out loud before the sort.

11Reframe · Card sort · tasks · 15 min

Sort eight tasks. Automate, assist or keep human.

Let tables argue for one minute per card. Assist means AI drafts and a person finishes. Keep human means the judgment is the work: acceptance decisions and client negotiations.

12Reframe · Your turn 1.1 · 10 min

Your task list, filtered

Ten minutes. People can use paper or prompt 1.3. Remind them: no client names in any tool. Collect two candidate tasks per table on the board; they come back in block 3.

13Design and select · Block 2 · Agent Design Framework · 2 min

An agent is a job description, not a chat.

Callback: the weak and designed instructions from the opener. The designed one was a job description.

14Design and select · Input · Instruction · Output · 10 min

Input · Instruction · Output.

Ask: in the weak instruction from the opener, which of the three was missing? Answer: all three were vague.

15Design and select · Instruction player · 15 min

Watch the instruction build, one part at a time.

Press → for each part. At each part ask: what would go wrong without this line? At Result, check one row live in the PDF (page 1, the license date). Decision: the agent never accepts a client.

16Design and select · Five parts · 10 min

Five parts of a strong instruction.

The weak column is what most people type. Each strong line removes one way the agent can guess.

17Design and select · Quick check · 5 min

The agent cannot find a shareholder's ID copy in the pack. What should a good instruction make it do?

Hands up for each option first, then click. Link it to the IF UNSURE part of the player.

18Design and select · Three test cases · 8 min

Three test cases, written before you run them.

Give an audit example of a trap: a movement that is large in percent but small in KWD.

19Design and select · Your turn 1.2 · 20 min

Fill one Agent Card

Twenty minutes: 14 to fill, 6 to swap. The test in the swap: could a new joiner run the card without asking a question? Common gap: the output is 'a summary'. Push for named columns.

20Design and select · Block 3 · Use case selection · 1 min

Pick one task. Build it well.

Keep this block short. The team choice matters more than the debate.

21Design and select · Four agents · 10 min

Four agents, four kinds of work.

Read each card: input, then output. Ask which one is closest to each table's work.

22Design and select · Teams · 9 min

Choose your agent and form your team.

Balance the teams: no team larger than five. Mix audit and onboarding people where you can.

23Build · Block 4 · Build in Claude · 2 min

A Claude Project is an agent you can reuse.

Welcome back from lunch. Callback: each team has an agent and a card. Now we build it.

24Build · Four parts of a project · 10 min

Four parts of a Claude Project, mapped to your card.

Show where Projects, project instructions and project knowledge are in Claude before the demo.

25Build · Live demo · 20 min

Live: build the KYC intake checker.

Build it live at normal speed; do not prepare it before. At step 5, ask someone to read the date from the PDF. Fallback: the expected output is on the staff backups page.

26Build · Your turn 1.3 · 40 min

Build sprint 1: your agent, version 1

Forty minutes. Prompts 1.5 to 1.8 are the four starter instructions. KYC team: their case is jabriya-kyc-pack.pdf, not the Khiran pack from the demo. Walk the room: check that the knowledge file is in the project, not just in one chat.

27Test and safety · Block 5 · Test and improve · 2 min

When the answer is wrong, fix the instruction, not the answer.

Ask one team what their agent got wrong in version 1.

28Test and safety · Read like a reviewer · 8 min

Four checks on every output.

These four checks are the reviewer's job. They take two minutes when the output is a table.

29Test and safety · Your turn 1.4 · 40 min

Build sprint 2: three tests, one fix

Forty minutes. Each team runs its three tests (see the answer key for what each agent should catch). Prompt 1.9 helps find weak lines. Teams keep a short test log: case, expected, actual, fixed.

30Test and safety · Card sort · safe use · 15 min

Green, amber or red? Sort before you paste.

Amber means: only in the approved account, with the minimum data, and a person checks the output. If someone asks what the firm allows, the answer is the firm's policy; we do not decide it here.

31Test and safety · Evidence you still test · 10 min

AI output is a first pass, not audit evidence.

Link to the Safe-use card on the site. This is the line to repeat on Demo Day.

32Close · Show three agents · 25 min

Three agents on the big screen.

Ask for three volunteer teams, about seven minutes each. Thank each team for one specific thing.

33Close · Close · 10 min

Your agent waits for you. Tomorrow it doesn't.

Ask each person to write one line: the task they would automate first at work. It comes back on Day 2.

Day 2 · Mon 16 Nov 2026 · 08:30-16:30

Turning Agents into Operational Workflows

32 slides · about 392 minutes of slide time · breaks and lunch as in the run of show.

01Opening · Title · 1 min

AI Innovation and Automation Day 2 · Turning Agents into Operational Workflows

On screen as people arrive. Check that everyone can sign in to n8n and that the workshop credential is ready to share.

02Opening · Callback · 7 min

Your agent works. But it waits for you.

Ask one team to say how many cases a week their agent could handle. Multiply by the five steps.

03Opening · Outcomes · 2 min

By the end of Day 2, you will be able to…

Point at the last line: Demo Day and the next task to automate.

04Opening · Roadmap · 5 min

One skeleton. Every agent fits it.

Share the workshop key the way the facilitator kit says. Never on the screen, never in the chat of a public channel.

05System · Block 1 · From agent to system · 2 min

A workflow is an agent the work can start.

Define the three words on the next three slides. Keep it concrete.

06System · The skeleton · 12 min

Trigger → Read → AI → Decide → Output.

Press → to light each step. Map each step to the Day 1 agent: Read and AI are what Claude did; Trigger and Decide are new.

07System · Triggers · 9 min

Four triggers you already know.

Ask: which trigger fits your team's agent? Most will say file or email. Today we practice with the form.

08System · Integrations · 9 min

Integrations: where the work already lives.

An integration is a ready-made connection to another system. n8n has hundreds. Connecting firm systems is an IT decision.

09System · Quick check · 5 min

In a KYC workflow, where should the human check sit?

Callback to Day 1: a person makes every acceptance decision.

10First build · Block 2 · Your first n8n workflow · 2 min

Three steps, one form, one answer.

Make sure everyone is signed in to n8n before you start the timer.

11First build · n8n in one screen · 12 min

Seven words you need today.

Show each one on the live screen in n8n, in this order. Create the Anthropic credential together with the workshop key.

12First build · Expressions · 10 min

Expressions move data from one step to the next.

Demo the drag live. Most errors today will be a field name that does not match exactly.

13First build · Your turn 2.1 · 20 min

Build: form → Claude → answer page

Twenty minutes. Common errors: no credential selected in the chat model; the field name does not match. When it works, ask them to open Executions and find their run.

14KYC build · Block 3 · Workflow build · 2 min

Connect input → AI → output, for real work.

Everyone builds KYC, whatever their team's agent is. It is the skeleton for the afternoon.

15KYC build · The KYC workflow · 7 min

Six nodes, one decision.

Press → to light each node. Parts: 1 is the first two nodes, 2 is the chain, 3 is the rest.

16KYC build · Named fields · 8 min

Ask for fields, not paragraphs.

The example uses a made-up client so it does not give away today's answers.

17KYC build · Your turn 2.2 · 20 min

KYC workflow, part 1: form and file

Twenty minutes. The binary field name: open the Binary tab in the trigger's output and copy the name exactly.

18KYC build · Your turn 2.3 · 25 min

KYC workflow, part 2: Claude decides

Twenty-five minutes. Reminder: n8n has no project knowledge, so the policy rules are in prompt 2.2. If the parser fails, the most common cause is a missing comma in the JSON example.

19KYC build · Your turn 2.4 · 25 min

KYC workflow, part 3: route and record

Twenty-five minutes. Create the two data tables first, with the six columns from prompt 2.3. Run both packs through the form, not the test button, so people see the real experience.

20KYC build · Quick check · 3 min

Salwa Trading lands in KYC ready. What do you do before you trust that route?

This is the Day 1 trap test, inside a workflow.

21Customize · Block 4 · Customization · 2 min

Same skeleton, your department's job.

Welcome back from lunch. Teams sit together for the whole afternoon.

22Customize · Swap three things · 10 min

Change the input, the instruction and the route.

Show the Aggregate node for the TB team: without it, Claude runs once for every row of the trial balance.

23Customize · Department needs · 8 min

What changes for audit and for onboarding.

PEP means politically exposed person. Screening is not something the workshop workflow does.

24Customize · Your turn 2.5 · 60 min

Team build: your agent as a workflow

Sixty minutes. Prompts 2.4 to 2.6 are the team versions; the KYC team adds jabriya-kyc-pack.pdf as a third test. At 40 minutes, every team must have one full run, even without the stretch.

25Demo and next · Block 5 · Demo Day · 1 min

Show it running. Then say what is next.

Set the running order now.

26Demo and next · Four beats · 5 min

A 3-minute demo in four beats.

Show the demo card, prompt 2.7.

27Demo and next · Your turn 2.6 · 25 min

Prepare your demo

Twenty-five minutes. Make every team rehearse once with the timer. The driver runs the workflow; the presenter talks.

28Demo and next · Demo Day · 45 min

Demo Day: every team, six minutes.

Use the 6-minute timer for each team. Note one strong point per team for the close.

29Demo and next · Before real work · 8 min

Before any agent touches real work.

This is the slide to photograph. It is also in the take-home guide.

30Demo and next · What to automate next · 7 min

What to automate next: score it, then pick one.

Callback: the line each person wrote at the end of Day 1.

31Demo and next · MAP test · after · 15 min

Scan the QR code. 10 minutes. Then the survey.

Start the timer. When people finish, they scan the second code for the final survey. Fallback: /post and /survey.

32Demo and next · Close · 20 min

Input, instruction, output. Then a trigger.

Name one strong point from each team's demo. Thank RSM's training team and the room.

Troubleshooting

What usually goes wrong

SymptomFix
Claude ignores the policyThe file is in one chat, not in the project knowledge. Add it to the project.
The agent invents a dateThe IF UNSURE part is missing or vague. Add: write Not found, never guess.
n8n: credential error in the chat modelNo credential selected, or the key was pasted with a space. Create it again.
n8n: Extract from File finds no fileThe input binary field name does not match. Copy it from the Binary tab of the trigger's output.
n8n: the parser failsThe JSON example has a missing comma or quote. Paste prompt 2.2's example again.
n8n: the TB chain runs 30 timesAggregate is missing between Extract from File and the chain.
n8n: IF always goes falseThe text must match exactly: Needs review, same capitals. Check the output field.
