Task 2.5Claude · scheduled tasksYour turn · 37 min

Clean runs, a failed run and the script

  1. Clean runs (10 min): copy your pack from Packs into your team's Inbox and run on demand, three times. Workflow teams: move the Outputs trackers to Processed first.
  2. Failed run (7 min): add the Fallback line (prompt sheet, Task 2.5) to the task's prompt. Copy mirqab-locked-test-file.pdf into the Inbox, run, read the log, delete it.
  3. Impact (4 min): your two times from lab step 5, and the formula on the impact slide.
  4. Demo plan (6 min): the Demo 2.6 prompt from the prompt sheet. Say who acts when a run fails.
  5. Rehearse once with a timer (10 min). Then copy a pack into the Inbox for the live run.
You hand in

A Log folder with three clean runs and the failed-run test, and a 5-minute demo, rehearsed once, with its impact number.

The fallback line (add it to your prompt)
If a file cannot be read, do not guess. Write Could not read: [file name] in the run log, leave the file in the Inbox and go on. A person checks it by hand.
Impact
From our run log, count the runs and the items flagged. Then calculate: ([minutes by hand] minus [minutes with the agent]) times [runs a month]. Our two times come from Task 1.10.

  • List the next two tasks your team would automate.
