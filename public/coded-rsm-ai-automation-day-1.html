Your turn

10 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 1.1 · 7 min · on your own

First Claude chat

  1. Open claude.ai. Start a new chat and attach abdali-engagement-letter-2026.docx with the + button.
  2. Ask the three questions below, one at a time.
  3. Check one answer against the letter yourself.
7:00 on the clock · Claude
You hand in: Three answers from Claude, one of them ticked against the letter.
Look for: Compare Claude's dates with the Timetable section of the letter.
Finished early? Ask Claude which paragraph each date came from, then check two of them.

Your turn · Task 1.2 · 18 min · on your own, then in pairs

Same task, 18 answers

  1. Start a new chat. Attach the invoice pack and the October purchase ledger.
  2. Write your own request: a table that checks each invoice against the ledger. You have 7 minutes.
  3. Compare your table with your neighbor's: columns, totals, and what each of you flagged.
  4. Be ready to show your table on the screen.
18:00 on the clock · Claude chat
You hand in: Your own reconciliation table, compared with a neighbor's.
Look for: Check every row against both files: number, date and amount.
Finished early? Run the same request again in a new chat. Compare the two tables.
abdali-supplier-invoices-oct-2026.pdfabdali-purchase-ledger-oct-2026.xlsx
Your turn · Task 1.3 · 12 min · on your own

Your task inventory

  1. Open the Day 1 lab page. List 10 tasks from your last week.
  2. Tag each one: repeats weekly, clear rules, document in and document out.
  3. Use the prompt card for your role (on the lab page). Pick the 3 tasks with three tags.
  4. Post your strongest task on the shared board, under your department.
12:00 on the clock · The Day 1 lab page
You hand in: 10 tasks tagged, your top 3, and the strongest one on the shared board.
Look for: If a task needs a meeting or a judgment call, it is not your first agent.
Finished early? Write the minutes per week for each of your top 3.
Your turn · Task 1.4 · 20 min · on your own, with the trainer

Build the invoice agent with me

  1. In claude.ai, open Projects and click + New Project. Name it Mirqab invoice match.
  2. Click Set project instructions. Paste the instructions below. Save.
  3. Add mirqab-invoice-match-template.xlsx to the project knowledge with +.
  4. Start a chat in the Project. Attach the October invoices and ledger. Type the run line.
20:00 on the clock · Claude Projects
You hand in: A Project named Mirqab invoice match and a table that flags both problems.
Look for: Check the Status column: at least two rows should not say Matched.
Finished early? Run the same request in a second chat inside the Project. Compare the two tables.

Your turn · Task 1.5 · 20 min · on your own

Write your Agent Card v1

  1. Score your 3 candidates on the four points (5 min).
  2. Pick one and place it in a track: Extraction, Analysis, Workflow or Reporting (2 min).
  3. Write your Agent Card on the Day 1 lab page: Input, Instruction, Output. Leave When empty (8 min).
  4. Paste the card on the shared board for approval (5 min).
20:00 on the clock · The Day 1 lab page
You hand in: An approved Agent Card v1 on the shared board.
Look for: Read your Instruction box as if you were new. Would you know what to do?
Finished early? Write a second card for your runner-up task.
Your turn · Task 1.6 · 65 min · on your own

Build v1 of your agent

  1. Create a Project named after your task. Write the instructions in five parts, from the template below.
  2. Add your template or one example output to the knowledge.
  3. Check 3 items by hand first. Extraction: leases 1, 4, 6. Analysis: Trade receivables, Accrued expenses, Bank charges. Workflow: items 5, 7, 8. Reporting: the 3 engagements.
  4. Run the agent on your pack. Compare those 3 items with yours. Fix the instructions, not the answer.
65:00 on the clock · Claude Projects
You hand in: Your own Project, right on the three items you checked by hand.
Look for: Hints in order: reread your Instruction box; use the five-part template; open the worked Project for your track.
Finished early? Add a second example output to the knowledge. Run again and check the same three items.

Your turn · Task 1.8 · 20 min · on your own

Fixed-shape output

  1. Add an output rule to your Project: exact columns, order, a total line.
  2. Run it twice on your track pack, each time in a new chat in the Project.
  3. Compare the two results. Fix the rule until they match in shape.
20:00 on the clock · Claude · artifacts
You hand in: One artifact or Excel file, in the same shape on two runs.
Look for: Name the columns in the order you want them. Claude follows the order you write.
Finished early? Run it on the messy file for your track. Check that the shape still holds.

Your turn · Task 1.9 · 20 min · on your own

Make your skill

  1. Code execution is on for the team: the owner turned it on. If your skill does not save, tell the trainer.
  2. In your Project, ask Claude to turn your instructions into a skill. Save it.
  3. Open a new chat outside the Project. Attach your pack and ask for the task in one line.
  4. Check that Claude uses your skill and returns the same shape.
  5. Use the transfer map. Add one line to your Agent Card: where this skill would live in ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini.
20:00 on the clock · Claude · skills
You hand in: Your skill, saved and tested in a fresh chat, and one transfer line on your card.
Look for: A skill needs a clear name and one line on when to use it.
Finished early? Share the skill with a member of your team.

Your turn · Task 1.10 · 14 min · on your own

The messy sample and the clock

  1. Download the messy file for your track. Run your skill on it. Workflow track: attach mirqab-pbc-request-list-abdali.xlsx with the messy email.
  2. Check: did the agent flag the problem, or guess? Each file hides a gap the agent must flag (the invoice file has two). No file has two values that conflict.

You hand in: One flagged messy run and two numbers: minutes by hand and with the agent.
Look for: A missing field or a different unit.
Finished early? Add the fix as a rule to your skill and rerun.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain why AI can give a confident figure that is wrong, and tick it against the source.

  • Build a Claude Project that gives the same, checkable table every run.
  • Read from a Drive folder and return a fixed-shape output.
  • Package your method as a skill you can share.
