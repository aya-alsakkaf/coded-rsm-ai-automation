Safe-use card

Sort the data before you paste it. The firm's IT and risk policy decides what is allowed. This card is a starting point, not the policy.

Green: fine with an approved tool

No client or personal data.

  • The fictional practice files from this workshop
  • Public information, for example a published annual report
  • The firm's blank templates and checklists
  • Your own notes with no client names

Amber: only with care

Only in the firm-approved account, with the minimum data, and a person checks the output.

  • A client's trial balance or ledger extract
  • Draft management letter wording, with no names
  • Client documents the firm's policy allows in that tool

Red: never

Do not paste, upload or connect.

  • ID copies, passport scans and personal ID numbers
  • Bank account numbers, card numbers and passwords
  • Any client file in a personal AI account
  • Anything the client or the policy marks confidential and not allowed
Before you paste
Three questions
  1. Is this tool approved by the firm for this data?
  2. Do I need all of this data, or only part of it?
  3. Who checks the output before anyone uses it?
After the AI answers
AI output is a first pass
  • AI output is a first pass, not audit evidence. Trace three values to the source.
  • Keep the instruction version and the test log.
  • A person makes every acceptance decision and every audit judgment.
