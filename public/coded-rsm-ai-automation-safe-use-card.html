Safe-use card

Before you put anything into an AI tool, sort the task. RSM's AI and data policy always comes first.

Green, amber, red Three risks RSM policy first

Green: go ahead

Fictional or public data, and your own drafting.

  • The Mirqab and Abdali sample files
  • A public standard or a published article
  • Rewriting your own short email
  • Your own task list, with no client or firm names

Amber: RSM-approved account only

RSM internal documents that hold no client data, in an account RSM approved, with names removed.

  • An internal checklist or template
  • An internal procedure or training note

Red: never, unless RSM approves it

Client data and personal data, in any AI tool RSM has not approved.

  • A client's ledger or trial balance
  • KYC documents and IDs
  • Client names in a prompt
  • Draft financial statements
Three risks, three habits
RiskWhat it looks likeThe habit
Wrong figuresA confident table with one figure from the wrong document.Tick every figure against the source, as you would a junior's working paper.
Client data leaving the firmA real ledger in a personal or free account.Sort the task first: green, amber or red. RSM's policy decides.
Automation without reviewA scheduled output that nobody reads.Drafts only, a named reviewer, and a run log you read every morning.
RSM's policy comes first
  • Ask IT or Risk Management which AI tools and accounts RSM approves.
  • If there is no approved account, amber becomes red.
  • Engagement confidentiality applies to AI tools exactly as it applies to email.
  • When you are not sure, treat the task as red and ask first.
