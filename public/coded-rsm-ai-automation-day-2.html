Turning Agents into Operational Workflows

Finish your agent with a fixed output and a skill. Then give your team's agent a When: a schedule, new inputs, department rules, a run log and a named reviewer. Show it at Demo Day.

Day 2 of 2 · Monday 16 Nov 08:30-16:30 In person · CODED, Alsharekh classroom (Frontend Campus)
The day in one line · 08:30-16:30

Day brief

Day 2 starts by finishing yesterday's build: a fixed-shape output, a second kind of artifact, and two skills. Then one question: who runs the agent when you are on leave? Your team turns one agent into a scheduled workflow that reads new files, follows your department's rules, writes a run log and waits for a person's review. Each team shows its workflow at Demo Day.

You build today: Your fixed-shape output and your skills, then your team's scheduled workflow, shown at Demo Day with its impact number.
