Turning Agents into Operational Workflows

Give your team's agent a When: a schedule, new inputs, department rules, a run log and a named reviewer. Then show it at Demo Day.

Day brief

Day 2 answers one question: who runs the agent when you are on leave? Your team turns one Day 1 agent into a scheduled workflow that reads new files, follows your department's rules, writes a run log and waits for a person's review. Each team shows its workflow at Demo Day.

You build today: Your team's scheduled workflow, shown at Demo Day with its impact number.

5 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 2.1 · 20 min · in your team

Your team's workflow card

  1. Agree on your team's agent (the board vote).
  2. Fill the workflow card on the Day 2 lab page: When, Input, Skill, Output, Who reviews.
  3. Post it on the shared board.
20:00 on the clock · The Day 2 lab page
You hand in: Your team's workflow card on the shared board.
Look for: Who reviews is one person's name.
Finished early? Write the one-line format of your run log.
Your turn · Task 2.2 · 30 min · in your team

Schedule your workflow

  1. Work on the laptop of the person who made your team's skill. That person also makes the Task 2.4 edits.
  2. Copy your pack from the Packs folder into [your team folder] / Inbox. Then click Scheduled in the left sidebar, then New task, then Set up manually.
  3. Name it. Paste the prompt below and fill the brackets from your card.
  4. Approval mode: choose the mode that lets the run save and move files without waiting for you.
  5. Frequency: daily at 08:00. Save.
  6. Run it once on demand. Compare the output with your Day 1 result.
30:00 on the clock · Claude · scheduled tasks
You hand in: A scheduled task, run once, with its output checked against Day 1.
Look for: Most failures are a folder name that does not match the Drive exactly.
Finished early? Add your team name to the output file name. Copy the pack from Packs into the Inbox again, run once more and check the new name.
Your turn · Task 2.3 · 25 min · in your team

Batch 2 and the empty folder

  1. Check that the empty-folder line is in your scheduled task's prompt.
  2. Workflow teams: keep the request list in the skill and add the tracker line below to the prompt. Analysis teams: add the monthly line below.
  3. Copy the batch-2 file for your track from Packs into your team's Inbox. Run the task on demand. Check the output and the run log.
  4. Run again with the Inbox empty. Check the run log.
25:00 on the clock · Claude · scheduled tasks
You hand in: One run on batch 2 and one empty-folder run, each with its own run-log file.
Look for: The run log after each run.
Finished early? Copy the batch-2 file into the Inbox again. Make the first line of the output a count of files read. Run on demand and check it against the run log.

Your turn · Task 2.5 · 40 min · in your team

Clean runs, a failed run and the script

  1. Copy your track pack into the Inbox and run on demand, three times. Workflow teams: move the trackers in Outputs to Processed before each run (12 min).
  2. Failed-run test: the skill owner adds the fallback line to the prompt. Copy mirqab-locked-test-file.pdf from Packs into the Inbox, run on demand and read the run log. Then delete it from the Inbox (8 min).
  3. Work out the impact: minutes by hand minus minutes with the agent, times runs a month (4 min).
  4. Write the 5-minute demo plan with the Demo 2.6 prompt. Say who acts when a run fails (8 min).
  5. Rehearse once, with a timer. Before Demo Day, copy a pack into the Inbox for the live run (8 min).
40:00 on the clock · Claude · scheduled tasks
You hand in: A Log folder with three clean runs and the failed-run test, and a rehearsed 5-minute demo with its impact number.
Look for: Every number on your demo comes from your run log or the Clock tab.
Finished early? List the next two tasks your team would automate.
