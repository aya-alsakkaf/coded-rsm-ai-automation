Your turn

6 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · 2.1 · 20 min

Build: form → Claude → answer page

  1. Create a new workflow. Add the trigger On form submission with one field.
  2. Add a Basic LLM Chain with the Anthropic Chat Model and the workshop credential.
  3. Add a Form Ending page that shows Claude's answer. Test it.
20:00 on the clock · N8N
What to look for: You open the form, type a question, and the answer page shows Claude's reply.
Your turn · 2.2 · 20 min

KYC workflow, part 1: form and file

  1. Add the trigger On form submission with two fields: Client name and KYC pack (file).
  2. Upload khiran-kyc-pack.pdf through the test form.
  3. Add Extract from File (Extract From PDF). Run it and read the text.
20:00 on the clock · N8N
What to look for: The pack's text appears in the output panel of Extract from File.
khiran-kyc-pack.pdf
Your turn · 2.3 · 25 min

KYC workflow, part 2: Claude decides

  1. Add a Basic LLM Chain with prompt 2.2 and your Day 1 instruction.
  2. Attach the Anthropic Chat Model with the workshop credential.
  3. Turn on a fixed output format with a Structured Output Parser. Run it.
25:00 on the clock · N8N
What to look for: The output has named fields: client, status, issues, summary.
khiran-kyc-pack.pdf
Your turn · 2.4 · 25 min

KYC workflow, part 3: route and record

  1. Create two data tables: KYC review queue and KYC ready.
  2. Add an IF on status, then one insert row step on each route.
  3. Add a Form Ending with the summary. Run both packs from the form.
25:00 on the clock · N8N
What to look for: One pack lands in the review queue and the other lands in the ready list. Check that each one is right.
khiran-kyc-pack.pdfsalwa-kyc-pack.pdf
Your turn · 2.5 · 60 min

Team build: your agent as a workflow

  1. Copy the KYC workflow. Rename it after your agent.
  2. Swap three things: the input, the instruction, the output fields and route.
  3. Test with the practice files. Stretch: add a second route or a schedule.
60:00 on the clock · N8N
What to look for: Your team's workflow runs from start to finish on a practice file, with a human check before anything leaves.
