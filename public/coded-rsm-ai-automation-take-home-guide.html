Take-home guide

Repeat what you built, safely. Start with dummy or green data. RSM's AI and data policy comes first.

Your own work, safely First week, first month RSM policy first
01

Your first week

  1. Pick your top task from the inventory. Write its Agent Card.
  2. Build it in an RSM-approved tool, on dummy data first.
  3. Test it on three cases where you know the answer.
02

Your first month

  1. Run it every week. Log the minutes by hand and with the agent.
  2. Add your department's rules and a review step.
  3. Share the skill with one colleague and ask them to find where it fails.
03

In any tool

  1. Use the transfer map: the same card becomes a ChatGPT Project, a Copilot agent or a Gemini Gem.
  2. Keep the five-part instruction. It works in every assistant.
  3. Schedule it only after it passes your tests.
!The habit · every time. Claude does the first pass. You review and sign off. RSM's AI and data policy comes first.
